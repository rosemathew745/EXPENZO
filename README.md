Project Description

Expenzo is a web-based financial management application that helps users track their monthly expenses, manage budgets efficiently, visualize spending analytics

The Problem Statement

Students and beginners often struggle with:

Tracking their daily and monthly expenses

Staying within a fixed budget

Understanding how stock trading works

Visualizing their spending behavior clearly

Most available tools are either too complex or lack educational simulation features.

The Solution

Expenzo provides:

A clean and easy-to-use expense tracker

Monthly budget management with alerts

Real-time remaining balance calculation

Pie chart visualization of expenses

A built-in stock market simulator with buy/sell functionality

Dynamic portfolio and total wealth tracking

This makes financial management interactive, simple, and educational.
